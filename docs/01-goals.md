# Project Goals

## Problem
Every year I help my realtor prepare to file taxes by organizing receipts and invoices into categories and cross referencing them with bank statements for duplicates. This process can be very time consuming and daunting.

## User
Business owners

## Goals
Unmatched items on both sides are visible within one click of importing

## Stretch Goals
- OCR
- Bank connections
- AI tax advice

## Success Criteria
My realtor reconciles one real month faster than by hand
